Appendix C

Standards crosswalk for compliance teams

The table maps the control plane’s main controls to three frameworks that compliance and audit teams report against:

  • ISO/IEC 42001 (its Annex A control objectives), with ISO/IEC 42005 for impact assessments.72
  • The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0 subcategories).73
  • The EU AI Act’s requirements for high-risk systems, which apply from December 2, 2027 for Annex III systems.32

The mapping is the author’s and indicative, so confirm clause-level mappings against the current texts with the compliance team.

Singapore’s Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI covers the same ground in four dimensions: assess and bound the risks up front, make humans meaningfully accountable, implement technical controls and processes, and enable end-user responsibility.75 The Cloud Security Alliance’s AI Controls Matrix maps its control objectives to ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act, and splits responsibilities among model providers, orchestrated service providers, application providers, AI customers and cloud providers, so an enterprise that runs its own control plane will usually read the matrix’s guidance for AI customers and for orchestrated service providers.74
