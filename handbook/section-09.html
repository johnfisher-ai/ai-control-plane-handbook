Section 9

Measuring success

Treat the control plane as a product with an owner, a plan and a scorecard. Report the scorecard monthly to the executive committee and quarterly to the AI governance board.29

The scorecard’s metrics are outcomes a board can read, and they move slowly. Delivery teams also need early signals that move within weeks, defined so that every team computes them the same way. Those signals should follow the agent’s whole trajectory as well as its final answer, checking that it chose the right tools, in the right order and at an acceptable cost.60 They should also measure the value delivered, because adoption alone says little. In BCG’s 2026 survey of close to 12,000 employees and leaders, the share whose organizations had put agents into workflows rose from 13% to 30% in a year, yet half said their companies lack clear governance for teams of people and AI.88
